Questions? Sorted.

Quick answers.

Do I need a team before registering?

You can register solo and use the event’s team-matching session, but your final team must have 2–4 people. For now, enter a temporary team name and update it with the organizers when team matching opens.

Who can participate?

Students and early-career professionals aged 18 or older are welcome. Beginners are encouraged; you do not need hackathon experience.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop, charger, valid photo ID, and anything specific your project needs. Meals, snacks, workspace, and Wi-Fi are included.

Is the registration fee refundable?

Refund and cancellation terms will be provided by the event organizers before payment is collected. No payment is taken through this prototype page.

Do we need a finished project?

No. Start with an idea, build a working prototype during the event, and present what you learned. Projects are judged on usefulness, creativity, feasibility, and the clarity of your demo.

What can we win?

There will be prizes for standout projects and track winners. Prize details and judging criteria will be announced at kickoff.
